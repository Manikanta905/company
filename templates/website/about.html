How We Started

Built from the Ground Up

MK Tech Solutions was founded in 2014 by a group of software engineers who saw a gap in the market: businesses needed high-quality custom technology but couldn't always afford enterprise-tier agencies.

Starting with a small team of five in a shared co-working space, we took on our first client — a local logistics company that needed a route-optimization platform. That project led to referrals, and referrals led to growth.

Today, MK Tech Solutions employs over 40 professionals across software engineering, design, cloud infrastructure, and product management. We've shipped more than 150 products and serve clients across North America, Europe, and Asia.
